THE METHODOLOGY

Evidence first.
Always.

Designed to show not just what matches, but why. And just as importantly, what remains uncertain.

OUR PROCESS

Four simple steps.

A repeatable process, with a link back to the source.

01

Read the claim.

Identify one company, fiscal year, supported metric and USD amount. Ambiguous language is flagged rather than guessed.

02

Find the evidence.

Locate the matching financial figure in a curated, source-linked reference record.

03

Compare precisely.

Normalize millions and billions, account for stated rounding precision, and detect mismatched fiscal years.

04

Explain the outcome.

Display the source, expected value and comparison, or a clear reason human review is required.

HONEST ABOUT THE LIMITS

Transparency is part of accuracy.

EvidenceCheck is a functional portfolio prototype, not an accounting audit, investment advice or a general AI-accuracy benchmark. Today it supports only Apple, Microsoft and NVIDIA; five standard metrics; and a curated FY2023–FY2025 snapshot. Historical figures were manually collected from published filings. The prototype does not automatically retrieve filings, reconcile private documents, or certify a model's broader financial accuracy. Unsupported cases are flagged for human review.

Production extensions could include live SEC companyfacts/XBRL ingestion, citation-level document extraction, authenticated workspaces, broader evaluation tests and human-review approval flows.
