# Data provenance and consistency notes

The `reference_facts.json` file is generated by `build_reference.py`, which contains a 2025 historical snapshot transcribed from published issuer financial statements (not scraped at runtime).

All three documents include annual comparative statements for 2025, 2024 and 2023. The 45 resulting fact rows each identify the reporting company, fiscal year, fiscal year end, metric, exact USD amount (millions, except EPS), source title and direct URL.

| Issuer | Financial report | Statement section | Fiscal periods |
|---|---|---|---|
| Apple | [FY2025 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/320193/000032019325000079/aapl-20250927.htm) | Consolidated Statements of Operations | Sep 2025 / Sep 2024 / Sep 2023 |
| Microsoft | [FY2025 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/789019/000095017025100235/msft-20250630.htm) | Income Statements | Jun 2025 / Jun 2024 / Jun 2023 |
| NVIDIA | [FY2025 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1045810/000104581025000023/nvda-20250126.htm) | Consolidated Statements of Income | Jan 2025 / Jan 2024 / Jan 2023 |

**Caveat:** A single FY2025 form contains FY2023 and FY2024 comparative numbers. We have not cross-validated every prior-year number against original corresponding-year individual Form 10-Ks; comparative amounts and potential restatements are taken as presented in the FY2025 report. NVIDIA's per-share historical results are adjusted for the ten-for-one split reflected in that published annual statement. They must not be mixed with pre-split data.

No model invented or automatically extracted these 45 baseline facts; they are human-transcribed from the stated documents. The separate demo assertions were written for testing and a subset is deliberately wrong.

## Guardrails

- No PDF or XBRL file is fetched by the server at runtime.
- Monetary amounts must identify the unit and fiscal year; the engine does not guess.
- A `match` means consistent with this curated reference and displayed precision, not confirmed by an accounting auditor.
- If an input is not supported, the engine records human review rather than returning a false assurance.
